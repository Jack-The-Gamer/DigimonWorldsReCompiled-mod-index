# DW1 Un-Rigged Bonus Try

The Bonus Try slot machine at the gyms plays fair: each reel stops on the picture that is in the middle of the window when you press **X**.

In the original game it isn't fair. Before a round starts, the game picks how it will treat you, and in one of its moods it never lets you win: when the first two reels match and the third would match too, the third reel rolls one or two pictures past it. In the others it nudges reels onto a match for you. With this mod none of that happens: what you see when you press is what you get, both ways.

- Works in every gym's Bonus Try (both training overlays).
- Payouts are unchanged: three of the training's own picture, three of a kind, or the jackpot.

Tested in Digimon World (USA).
