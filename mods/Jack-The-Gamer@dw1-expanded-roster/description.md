# DW1 Expanded Roster

**Work in progress.** Digimon from Digimon World 2003 and Digimon World 2 join Digimon World, built from your own copies of those games each time the game starts (nothing of any of the games comes with the mod).

The first line is **Guilmon → Growlmon → WarGrowlmon**:

- **Guilmon** (Virus Rookie, Fire and Battle): raise a **Koromon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Speed** and keep it **well fed (weight 20 to 30)**, and it becomes Guilmon instead of Agumon or Gabumon. Its finisher is **Pyro Sphere**. Guilmon can become Growlmon, Greymon, Tyrannomon or Airdramon.
- **Growlmon** (Virus Champion, Fire and Battle): from Guilmon, aiming for **Offense and Speed 100**, at most **3 care mistakes**, weight **25 to 35** and **30 techniques**. Its finisher is **Exhaust Flame**. Growlmon can become WarGrowlmon, SkullGreymon or Megadramon.
- **WarGrowlmon** (Virus Ultimate, Fire, Mech and Battle): from **Growlmon, Greymon or Tyrannomon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense 500, Speed 500**, Defense and Brains 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 60 to 70, discipline 80, 30 battles and 30 techniques. Its finisher is **Atomic Blaster**, fired from its chest.

The second line is **Veemon**, which becomes **ExVeemon → Paildramon** or **Veedramon → AeroVeedramon** (Veedramon and AeroVeedramon come from Digimon World 2; ExVeemon can also reach AeroVeedramon):

- **Veemon** (Vaccine Rookie): raise a **Tsunomon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Brains** at **weight 15 to 25**. Its finisher is **V-Headbutt**: Veemon dashes into the foe with Buster Dive's impact. Veemon can become Veedramon, ExVeemon or Seadramon.
- **ExVeemon** (Vaccine Champion): from Veemon, aiming for **Offense and Speed 100**, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 20 to 30, 10 battles and 30 techniques. Its finisher is **V-Laser** (with Howling Blaster's beam, from its chest). ExVeemon can become Paildramon, AeroVeedramon or Mamemon.
- **Veedramon** (Vaccine Champion): from Veemon, aiming for **Offense and Defense 100**, Brains 80, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 25 to 35, discipline 70 and 30 techniques. Its finisher is **V-Nova Blast** (with Howling Blaster's beam, from its mouth). Veedramon can become MetalGreymon or AeroVeedramon.
- **Paildramon** (Vaccine Ultimate): from ExVeemon, Kabuterimon or Airdramon, aiming for HP 3000, MP 2000, **Offense and Speed 400**, Defense and Brains 300, at most 8 care mistakes, weight 45 to 55, discipline 90, 30 battles and 35 techniques. Its finisher is **Death Parade Blaster** (with Giga Scissor Claw's blast).
- **AeroVeedramon** (Vaccine Ultimate): from Veedramon, ExVeemon, Greymon, Airdramon or Tyrannomon, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense 500**, Defense and Speed 400, Brains 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 55 to 65, discipline 90, 30 battles and 30 techniques. Its finisher is **Dragon Impulse** (Crimson Flare's fire, gathered on its wings and hands).

The third line brings **Tentomon** to you and adds **Megas**:

- **Tentomon** (Data Rookie, Earth): Digimon World's own Tentomon, until now only met in the wild. Raise a **Tanemon** whose highest stat is **Offense** at **weight 15 to 25** (Palmon and Betamon still come from MP, Speed or Brains and from HP, MP or Defense). Its finisher is **Super Shocker**, gathered on its four claws. Tentomon can become **Kabuterimon or Kuwagamon**, and cares like a Rookie (Kunemon's meals, sleep and weight).
- **MegaKabuterimon** (Vaccine Ultimate, from Digimon World 2): from **Kabuterimon or Kuwagamon**, aiming for **HP 5000, Defense 500**, Offense 400, Speed 300, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 50 to 60, 30 battles and 35 techniques. Its finisher is **Horn Buster**.
- **Okuwamon** (Virus Ultimate, from Digimon World 2003): from **Kabuterimon or Kuwagamon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense and Speed 500**, Defense 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 40 to 50, 30 battles and 30 techniques. It walks on all four legs with Centarumon's motions but keeps HerculesKabuterimon's insect techniques. Its finisher is **Double Scissor Claw**: it rushes the foe with Kuwagamon's Scissor Claw.
- **Garudamon** (Vaccine Ultimate, from Digimon World 2): from **Birdramon, Angemon or Kokatorimon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, Offense and Speed 400, Defense and Brains 300, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 35 to 45, discipline 90 and 35 techniques. Its finisher is **Wing Blade** (Meteor Wing's fire, from its wings and hands).
- **HerculesKabuterimon** and **Phoenixmon** are now **Megas**, with their own section on the Digivolution Chart. Champions no longer become them: an **Ultimate** becomes a Mega the way a Champion becomes an Ultimate, once it has been an Ultimate for **144 hours** (6 days) and comes close enough to the Mega's requirements.
  - **HerculesKabuterimon** comes from **MegaKabuterimon or Okuwamon**, aiming for HP 7000, MP 4000, Offense 600, **Defense 700**, Speed 500, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 55 to 65, 50 battles and 45 techniques.
  - **Phoenixmon** comes from **Garudamon or Piximon**, aiming for HP 6000, MP 7000, Offense and Defense 500, **Speed 700, Brains 700**, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 30 to 40, discipline 100 and 45 techniques.
  - Kabuterimon, Kuwagamon and Shellmon no longer become HerculesKabuterimon, and Birdramon, Angemon, Airdramon, Kokatorimon and Unimon no longer become Phoenixmon (Birdramon now becomes Garudamon).

As with the game's own Digimon, the evolution is decided by how close your partner comes to these, so it doesn't have to meet every one.

For now the new Digimon:

- move with the animations of Digimon World's own Digimon (Agumon, Greymon, MetalGreymon, Gabumon, Leomon, Andromon, Kabuterimon, HerculesKabuterimon, Centarumon), and use their techniques (Okuwamon uses HerculesKabuterimon's) (their finishers have their own names and effects);
- keep those Digimon's care habits and voices;
- don't show on the Digivolution Chart yet once raised (a save has no room to record them so far); the chart itself is rebuilt with room for them and a Mega section, and its detail page shows any number of evolutions and pre-evolutions.

**Needs** Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer, Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032), and **Digimon World 2003 (Europe, SLES-03936)** and **Digimon World 2 (USA, SLUS-01193)** imported in the launcher. Without one of them the game plays as usual, minus the Digimon from that game; the American and Japanese Digimon World 3 discs and the Japanese Digimon World 2 disc aren't supported yet.

Checked on Digimon World (USA): loading a save, the field, the menus and battles play exactly as without the mod; a Koromon at weight 25 with Offense highest becomes Guilmon (at weight 15 it still becomes Agumon); all twelve new Digimon evolve, show on the Digimon page and the Digivolution Chart, and use their finishers in battle; a Tanemon with Offense highest at weight 20 becomes Tentomon by itself; a MegaKabuterimon with HerculesKabuterimon's stats becomes it by itself; every new Digimon can evolve on (before this version the evolution scene could hang when a new Digimon evolved further).
