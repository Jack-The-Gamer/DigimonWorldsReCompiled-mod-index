# DW1 Expanded Roster

**Work in progress.** Digimon from Digimon World 2003 join Digimon World, built from your own copy of Digimon World 2003 each time the game starts (nothing of either game comes with the mod).

The first line is **Guilmon → Growlmon → WarGrowlmon**:

- **Guilmon** (Virus Rookie, Fire and Battle): raise a **Koromon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Speed** and keep it **well fed (weight 20 to 30)**, and it becomes Guilmon instead of Agumon or Gabumon. Its finisher is **Pyro Sphere**. Guilmon can become Growlmon, Greymon, Tyrannomon or Airdramon.
- **Growlmon** (Virus Champion, Fire and Battle): from Guilmon, aiming for **Offense and Speed 100**, at most **3 care mistakes**, weight **25 to 35** and **30 techniques**. Its finisher is **Exhaust Flame**. Growlmon can become WarGrowlmon or SkullGreymon.
- **WarGrowlmon** (Virus Ultimate, Fire, Mech and Battle): from **Growlmon, Greymon or Tyrannomon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense 500, Speed 500**, Defense and Brains 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 60 to 70, discipline 80, 30 battles and 30 techniques. Its finisher is **Atomic Blaster**, fired from its chest.

As with the game's own Digimon, the evolution is decided by how close your partner comes to these, so it doesn't have to meet every one.

For now the new Digimon:

- move with the animations of Agumon, Greymon and MetalGreymon, and use their techniques (their finishers have their own names and effects);
- keep those Digimon's care habits and voices;
- don't show on the Digivolution Chart yet once raised (a save has no room to record them so far); the chart itself is rebuilt with room for them, and for a future Mega column.

**Needs** Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer, Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032), and **Digimon World 2003 (Europe, SLES-03936)** imported in the launcher. Without Digimon World 2003 the game plays as usual; the American and Japanese Digimon World 3 discs aren't supported yet.

Checked on Digimon World (USA): loading a save, the field, the menus and battles play exactly as without the mod; a Koromon at weight 25 with Offense highest becomes Guilmon (at weight 15 it still becomes Agumon); all three new Digimon evolve, walk, show on the Digimon page and use their finishers in battle.
