# DW1 Expanded Roster

**Work in progress.** Digimon from Digimon World 2003 and Digimon World 2 join Digimon World, built from your own copies of those games each time the game starts (nothing of any of the games comes with the mod), along with Digimon World's own Tentomon and Machinedramon as Digimon you can raise, and a revised Digivolution tree with Megas.

## The new Digimon

- **Guilmon** (Virus Rookie, Fire and Battle): raise a **Koromon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Speed** at **weight 20 to 30**. Finisher: **Pyro Sphere**.
- **Veemon** (Vaccine Rookie): raise a **Tsunomon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Brains** at **weight 15 to 25**. Finisher: **V-Headbutt** (it dashes into the foe with Buster Dive's impact).
- **Tentomon** (Data Rookie, Earth; Digimon World's own, until now only met in the wild): raise a **Tanemon** whose highest stat is **Offense** at **weight 15 to 25**. Finisher: **Super Shocker**, gathered on its four claws. It cares like a Rookie (Kunemon's meals, sleep and weight).
- **Growlmon** (Virus Champion): from Guilmon, aiming for **Offense and Speed 100**, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 25 to 35 and 30 techniques. Finisher: **Exhaust Flame**.
- **ExVeemon** (Vaccine Champion): from Veemon, aiming for **Offense and Speed 100**, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 20 to 30, 10 battles and 30 techniques. Finisher: **V-Laser** (Howling Blaster's beam, from its chest).
- **Veedramon** (Vaccine Champion, from Digimon World 2): from Betamon or Veemon, aiming for **Offense and Defense 100**, Brains 80, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 25 to 35, discipline 70 and 30 techniques. Finisher: **V-Nova Blast** (Howling Blaster's beam, from its mouth).
- **Deltamon** (Virus Champion, Battle, Mech and Earth): from Veemon, aiming for **HP, Offense and Defense 100**, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 30 to 40, and 15 battles or 28 techniques. It walks with Tyrannomon's motions. Finisher: **Triplex Force** (Fireball's flames from its three mouths).
- **WarGrowlmon** (Virus Ultimate): from **Growlmon or Tyrannomon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense and Speed 500**, Defense and Brains 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 60 to 70, discipline 80, 30 battles and 30 techniques. Finisher: **Atomic Blaster**, fired from its chest.
- **Paildramon** (Vaccine Ultimate): from **ExVeemon or Kabuterimon**, aiming for HP 3000, MP 2000, **Offense and Speed 400**, Defense and Brains 300, at most 8 care mistakes, weight 45 to 55, discipline 90, 30 battles and 35 techniques. Finisher: **Death Parade Blaster**.
- **AeroVeedramon** (Vaccine Ultimate, from Digimon World 2): from **Airdramon, Veedramon, ExVeemon or Deltamon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense 500**, Defense and Speed 400, Brains 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 55 to 65, discipline 90, 30 battles and 30 techniques. Finisher: **Dragon Impulse** (Crimson Flare's fire, gathered on its wings and hands).
- **MegaKabuterimon** (Vaccine Ultimate, from Digimon World 2): from **Kabuterimon or Kuwagamon**, aiming for **HP 5000, Defense 500**, Offense 400, Speed 300, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 50 to 60, 30 battles and 35 techniques. Finisher: **Horn Buster**.
- **Okuwamon** (Virus Ultimate): from **Kuwagamon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, **Offense and Speed 500**, Defense 300, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 40 to 50, 30 battles and 30 techniques. It walks on all four legs with Centarumon's motions and keeps HerculesKabuterimon's insect techniques. Finisher: **Double Scissor Claw** (it rushes the foe with Kuwagamon's Scissor Claw).
- **Garudamon** (Vaccine Ultimate, from Digimon World 2): from **Angemon, Birdramon, Kokatorimon, Leomon or Meramon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 3000, Offense and Speed 400, Defense and Brains 300, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 35 to 45, discipline 90 and 35 techniques. Finisher: **Wing Blade** (Meteor Wing's fire, from its wings and hands).
- **Kimeramon** (Data Ultimate, Fire, Air and Battle): from **Airdramon, Angemon, Devimon, Garurumon, Greymon, Kabuterimon, Kuwagamon, Monochromon or Deltamon**, aiming for HP 4000, MP 4000, **Offense 450**, Defense, Speed and Brains 400, at most 15 care mistakes, weight 50 to 60, and 50 battles or 40 techniques. Finisher: **Heat Viper** (Crimson Flare's fire, gathered on its four hands).

## Megas

HerculesKabuterimon, Phoenixmon and **Machinedramon** are **Megas**, with their own section on the Digivolution Chart. An **Ultimate** becomes a Mega the way a Champion becomes an Ultimate, once it has been an Ultimate for **144 hours** (6 days) and comes close enough to the Mega's requirements.

- **Machinedramon** (Virus Mega, Mech, Fire and Battle): Digimon World's own last boss, now yours. From **Andromon, Megadramon, MetalGreymon, MetalMamemon or Kimeramon**, aiming for HP 8000, MP 5000, Offense 700, **Defense 800**, Speed 400, Brains 600, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 75 to 85, and 80 battles or 45 techniques. Its finisher is its own **Infinity Cannon**, with its own motion: the cannons swing forward over its shoulders and fire (at a Mega's power, not the boss's).
- **HerculesKabuterimon** comes from **MegaKabuterimon**, aiming for HP 7000, MP 4000, Offense 600, **Defense 700**, Speed 500, at most 5 care mistakes, weight 55 to 65, 50 battles and 45 techniques.
- **Phoenixmon** comes from **Garudamon**, aiming for HP 6000, MP 7000, Offense and Defense 500, **Speed 700, Brains 700**, at most 3 care mistakes, weight 30 to 40, discipline 100 and 45 techniques.

## The revised Digivolution tree

Every Digimon in the mod evolves along a revised tree, not the game's own. Some changes:

- **Koromon** becomes Agumon, Guilmon or **Biyomon**; **Tanemon** becomes Palmon, **Kunemon** (HP or Defense highest, weight 5 to 15) or Tentomon; **Tokomon** becomes Patamon or **Penguinmon**; **Tsunomon** becomes **Gabumon**, Veemon, **Betamon** or Elecmon (Elecmon now at weight 5 to 15).
- **Whamon** is an **Ultimate**, from Coelamon, Seadramon or Shellmon, aiming for HP 4000, Defense 400, Brains 400, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 45 to 55, and discipline 70 or 30 techniques.
- **Vademon** can be raised from **Nanimon**, aiming for MP 4000, Brains 500, at most 15 care mistakes, weight 15 to 25 and 35 techniques.
- Many Rookies and Champions have new or fewer evolutions (Agumon: Greymon, Monochromon, Tyrannomon; Greymon: MetalGreymon, SkullGreymon, Megadramon, Kimeramon; and so on), shown on the chart.

The tree also names Digimon that aren't in the mod yet (ToyAgumon, Gomamon, Renamon, Gatomon, MetalGarurumon and many more); they join it as they're added. Until then Bakemon, Coelamon and Ninjamon, which would otherwise only come from those, keep their old routes (from Elecmon or Kunemon, Betamon or Palmon, and Palmon), and Drimogemon, Unimon and Vegiemon keep their old Ultimates.

As with the game's own Digimon, the evolution is decided by how close your partner comes to these, so it doesn't have to meet every one.

For now the new Digimon:

- move with the animations of Digimon World's own Digimon (Agumon, Greymon, Tyrannomon, MetalGreymon, Gabumon, Leomon, Andromon, Kabuterimon, HerculesKabuterimon, Centarumon), and use their techniques (Okuwamon uses HerculesKabuterimon's) (their finishers have their own names and effects);
- keep those Digimon's care habits and voices;
- don't show on the Digivolution Chart yet once raised (a save has no room to record them so far); the chart itself is rebuilt with room for them and a Mega section, and its detail page shows any number of evolutions and pre-evolutions.

**Needs** Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer, Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032), and **Digimon World 2003 (Europe, SLES-03936)** and **Digimon World 2 (USA, SLUS-01193)** imported in the launcher. Without one of them the game plays as usual, minus the Digimon from that game; the American and Japanese Digimon World 3 discs and the Japanese Digimon World 2 disc aren't supported yet.

Checked on Digimon World (USA): loading a save, the field, the menus and battles play exactly as without the mod; all fifteen new Digimon evolve, show on the Digimon page and use their finishers in battle; a Koromon at weight 25 with Offense highest becomes Guilmon; a Tanemon with Offense highest at weight 20 becomes Tentomon; a MegaKabuterimon with HerculesKabuterimon's stats and a MetalGreymon with Machinedramon's become them by themselves; every new Digimon can evolve on.
