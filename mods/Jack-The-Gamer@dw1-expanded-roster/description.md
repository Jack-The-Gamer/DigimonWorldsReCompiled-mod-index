# DW1 Expanded Roster

**Work in progress.** Digimon from Digimon World 2003 and Digimon World 2 join Digimon World, built from your own copies of those games each time the game starts (nothing of any of the games comes with the mod), along with Digimon World's own Tentomon, Goburimon, Gotsumon, Hagurumon, ToyAgumon, Tankmon, PlatinumSukamon, WaruMonzaemon, KingSukamon and Machinedramon as Digimon you can raise, and a revised Digivolution tree with Megas.

## The new Digimon

- **Guilmon** (Virus Rookie, Fire and Battle): raise a **Koromon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Speed** at **weight 20 to 30**. Finisher: **Pyro Sphere**.
- **Veemon** (Vaccine Rookie, Battle): raise a **Tsunomon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Brains** at **weight 15 to 25**. Finisher: **V-Headbutt** (a real Buster Dive: it crouches and dives head first into the foe).
- **Tentomon** (Data Rookie, Air and Battle; Digimon World's own, until now only met in the wild): raise a **Tanemon** whose highest stat is **Offense** at **weight 15 to 25**. Finisher: **Super Shocker**, gathered on its four claws. It cares like a Rookie (Kunemon's meals, sleep and weight).
- **Goburimon** (Virus Rookie, Combat and Fire; Digimon World's own, until now only met as an enemy): raise a **Tanemon** whose highest stat is **Offense or Speed** at **weight 25 to 35**. It moves with Ogremon's motions, its club carried the same way. Finisher: **Goblin Strike** (a fireball hurled from its club).
- **Gotsumon** (Data Rookie, Earth and Combat; Digimon World's own): raise a **Tanemon** whose highest stat is **HP or Defense** at **weight 25 to 35**. It moves with Gabumon's motions. Finisher: **Rock Fist** (a punch that fires Volcanic Strike's rocks from its fist).

## Megas

An **Ultimate** becomes a Mega the way a Champion becomes an Ultimate, once it has been an Ultimate for **144 hours** (6 days) and comes close enough to the Mega's requirements.

- **Machinedramon** (Virus Mega, Mech, Fire and Battle): Digimon World's own last boss, now yours. From **Andromon, Megadramon, MetalGreymon, MetalMamemon or Kimeramon**, aiming for HP 8000, MP 5000, Offense 700, **Defense 800**, Speed 400, Brains 600, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 75 to 85, and 80 battles or 45 techniques. ## The revised Digivolution tree

Every Digimon in the mod evolves along a revised tree, not the game's own. Some changes:

- **Koromon** becomes Agumon, Guilmon, ToyAgumon, **Biyomon** or Hagurumon; **Tanemon** becomes Palmon, **Kunemon** (HP or Defense highest, weight 5 to 15), Tentomon, Gotsumon or Goburimon; **Tokomon** becomes Patamon or **Penguinmon**; **Tsunomon** becomes **Gabumon**, Veemon, **Betamon**, Elecmon (now at weight 5 to 15) or Renamon.
- **Whamon** is an **Ultimate**, from Coelamon, Seadramon or Shellmon, aiming for HP 4000, Defense 400, Brains 400, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 45 to 55, and discipline 70 or 30 techniques.
- **Devimon** can be raised from **Goburimon**, aiming for MP, Offense and Brains 100, **at least 10 care mistakes**, weight 25 to 35, and 30 battles.
- **Vademon** can be raised from **Nanimon**, aiming for MP 4000, Brains 500, at most 15 care mistakes, weight 15 to 25 and 35 techniques.
- Many Rookies and Champions have new or fewer evolutions (Agumon: Greymon, Monochromon, Tyrannomon; Greymon: MetalGreymon, SkullGreymon, Megadramon, Kimeramon; and so on), shown on the chart.

The tree also names Digimon that aren't in the mod yet (Gomamon, Gatomon, Mammothmon and many more); they join it as they're added. Until then Bakemon and Coelamon, which would otherwise only come from those, keep their old routes (from Elecmon or Kunemon, and Betamon or Palmon), and Drimogemon, Unimon and Vegiemon keep their old Ultimates.

As with the game's own Digimon, the evolution is decided by how close your partner comes to these, so it doesn't have to meet every one.

For now the new Digimon:

- move with the animations of Digimon World's own Digimon (Agumon, Greymon, Tyrannomon, MetalGreymon, Gabumon, Leomon, Andromon, Kabuterimon, HerculesKabuterimon, Centarumon, Sukamon, Shellmon, Garurumon, Angemon, Ogremon, Giromon, Monzaemon, Etemon, Monochromon), and play those motions for their techniques (their finishers have their own names and effects);
- keep those Digimon's care habits and voices;
- don't show on the Digivolution Chart yet once raised (a save has no room to record them so far); the chart itself is rebuilt with room for them and a Mega section, and its detail page shows any number of evolutions and pre-evolutions.

With **DW1 Wild Digimon Randomizer**, the new Digimon can also turn up as wild Digimon. On a map each one loads as a lighter copy with only what a wild Digimon uses (its walking and battle motions, its finisher and three other techniques), about half the size of the full Digimon, so it fits beside the area's other Digimon.

**Needs** Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer, Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032), and **Digimon World 2003 (Europe, SLES-03936)** and **Digimon World 2 (USA, SLUS-01193)** imported in the launcher. Without one of them the game plays as usual, minus the Digimon from that game; the American and Japanese Digimon World 3 discs and the Japanese Digimon World 2 disc aren't supported yet.

Checked on Digimon World (USA): loading a save, the field, the menus and battles play exactly as without the mod; all forty-one new Digimon evolve, show on the Digimon page and use their finishers in battle; a full virus bar and a Rookie with nothing to become each give the rarer Digimon about a quarter of the time; a Koromon at weight 25 with Offense highest becomes Guilmon; a Tanemon with Offense highest at weight 20 becomes Tentomon; a MegaKabuterimon with HerculesKabuterimon's stats and a MetalGreymon with Machinedramon's become them by themselves; every new Digimon can evolve on.
