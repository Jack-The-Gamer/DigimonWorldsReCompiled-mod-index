DW1 Expanded Roster 0.25.0: digivolution requirements
=====================================================

For Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032). Every Digimon you can raise, what it
comes from, what it aims for and what it becomes.

How a digivolution is chosen
----------------------------
Your partner tries to digivolve once it has spent enough hours at its level
(counted since its last digivolution):
  Fresh -> In-Training     6 hours (always the same In-Training)
  In-Training -> Rookie   24 hours
  Rookie -> Champion      72 hours (nothing met after 96 hours: Numemon, or
                                    Raremon a quarter of the time)
  Champion -> Ultimate   144 hours
  Ultimate -> Mega       144 hours as an Ultimate

Each Digimon it can become is scored, one point for each of:
  1. Stats: every stat listed is at least that high. For a Rookie it is
     instead which stat is your partner's highest (one of those listed).
  2. Care mistakes: within the limit.
  3. Weight: within the range (the target weight plus or minus 5).
  4. Bonus: any ONE of the bonus conditions.
A Digimon needs 3 of the 4 points, so your partner doesn't have to meet
everything. From an In-Training the highest score wins (on a tie, the one
listed first). From a Rookie, Champion or Ultimate, of the Digimon scoring
3 or 4 the one whose listed stats your partner has highest on average wins.
A Digimon you have already raised never takes the place of one you
haven't. That counts for the new Digimon too: the mod keeps which ones
you have raised with each save.

HP and MP are shown as on the Digimon page (the game compares them in tens).

Special digivolutions
---------------------
* A full virus bar turns your partner into Sukamon, or PlatinumSukamon a
  quarter of the time.
* A Sukamon or PlatinumSukamon that has eaten 20 piles of poop since it
  digivolved becomes KingSukamon at once.
* The game's own event digivolutions are unchanged.

Needs a Digimon World 2003 / Digimon World 3 disc (Europe, USA or Japan)
and a Digimon World 2 disc (USA or Japan) imported in the launcher for
every Digimon below; without one of them the Digimon from that game are
left out of the tree.
