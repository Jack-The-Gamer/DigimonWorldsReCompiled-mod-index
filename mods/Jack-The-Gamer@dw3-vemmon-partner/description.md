# DW3 Vemmon Partner

Vemmon joins your team as a ninth partner, and its line (Vemmon, Snatchmon, Destromon and Galacticmon) becomes something every partner can raise.

- **Getting Vemmon:** beat Vemmon in the story battle at the Admin Center to earn the Vemmon DDNA, then bring it to the new DRI Agent in Asuka City's Cargo Tower.
- **Vemmon as a partner:** it fights with its own battle model and its claw attack (Messer Claw), and appears in the field, the party and Lab menus, the save screens, the renaming screen and the Digivolve Chart. Its gym animations are drawn from its own 3D model when the game starts.
- **The Vemmon line:** Snatchmon, Destromon and Galacticmon, with 15 new Machine, Dark and Thunder techniques (names and descriptions in all five languages). Every partner's digivolution list gains the line.
- **Saving:** Vemmon and its progress are kept in your save, also when it leads the party.

This mod is **experimental**:

- Snatchmon's and Destromon's models are placeholders, and Galacticmon is the last boss's body phase shrunk to partner size.
- Vemmon uses Guilmon's Digivolve Chart recipes and equipment.
- A full playthrough (learning the forms by level, saving and loading them over a long game) hasn't been tested yet.

Made for Digimon World 2003 (Europe, SLES-03936) only. Everything it changes is read from your own disc while the game starts (adding a few seconds to loading); the mod ships only its own code and models.
