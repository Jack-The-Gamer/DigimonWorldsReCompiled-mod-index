# DW1 Wild Digimon Randomizer

The wild Digimon roaming the File Island maps become **other Digimon of the same stage that share at least two of their specialties**. A Dokunemon (Earth and Air) in Native Forest can turn into a Muchomon or a Kunemon, an IceDevimon in Freezeland into a Devimon, Garurumon or Rockmon, a Tekkamon in the Back Dimension into a Giromon, SkullGreymon or Machinedramon.

- **Same stage**: Rookies stay Rookies, Champions stay Champions, Ultimates stay Ultimates.
- **Shared specialties**: at least two in common. A wild Digimon with only one specialty (Goburimon is Battle only) is replaced by Digimon that have it too.
- **Their own techniques**: a replacement fights with its own moves. It keeps a technique the original also knew, otherwise it uses one of its own of the same kind, and its own finisher where the original had one.
- **Same difficulty**: HP, MP, stats and the bits you win stay those of the area, so the game's balance doesn't change.
- **Only wild Digimon**: story Digimon, bosses, recruits (such as the Coelamon at the Beach) and the Digimon in File City stay as they are.

**Options**

- **New Digimon**
  - **Every visit** (default): an area's wild Digimon are picked again each time you enter it.
  - **Fixed**: an area always has the same wild Digimon.
- **Include Expanded Roster Digimon** (default on): with DW1 Expanded Roster installed, its new Digimon can be picked too (see below).

**Notes**

- All the wild Digimon of one kind in an area become the same Digimon, as in the original game, where an area loads one model per kind.
- The PlayStation has little memory for the Digimon on a map. A replacement is only picked when its model fits next to everything else the area needs, so bigger Digimon turn up less often in crowded areas, and sometimes a wild Digimon stays itself. ClearAgumon always does: no other Rookie shares its specialties.
- Some of the game's Digimon exist only as cut-down copies made for one story scene (Kuwagamon's, Elecmon's, Leomon's and others). Those copies can't walk and fight like wild Digimon, so the mod uses the full versions of those Digimon instead, when they fit.
- **DW1 Expanded Roster**: the two mods work together in either order. The randomizer reads the Digimon tables where Expanded Roster moves them, and its new Digimon are part of the pool: a Guilmon pack in Toy Mansion, Renamon in Freezeland, Growlmon in Toy Town. On a map, Expanded Roster loads a lighter copy of each one, about the size of the game's own wild Digimon, with its finisher and three other techniques. With Expanded Roster 0.11.0 or older, which has no such copies, its Digimon rarely fit. Where Digimon World has its own model of a Digimon (Gotsumon, Goburimon, Tankmon...), the randomizer uses that one.

Made for Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032) and checked there, alone and with DW1 Expanded Roster: areas from Native Forest to the Back Dimension, battles against the replacements, and the game's check mode.

Needs Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer.
