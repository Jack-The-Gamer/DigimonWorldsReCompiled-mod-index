# DW1 Re-Usable Monzaemon Suit

Once Monzaemon joins the city, his suit stands in the side room of **Jijimon's House**. In the original game it only says "It's empty." With this mod, **examine it with Numemon as your partner and Numemon climbs in and digivolves into Monzaemon**, just like the suit in Toy Town. You can do it again whenever your partner is Numemon.

- With Numemon: "It's empty." and "There's a zipper on the back.", then Numemon gets in and the full digivolution scene plays. These are the game's own lines and scene, taken from the Toy Town event on your disc.
- With any other partner the suit still just says "It's empty."
- The suit shows up in the same way as before: after Monzaemon's Stuffed Animal joins the city.

**Options**

- **Hide the suit while Monzaemon is your partner** (default on): the suit isn't in the room while it's being worn, as in Toy Town. It's back once your partner is someone else. Turn this off to leave the suit in the room all the time.

**Notes**

- The mod changes the room's script when the game starts. Your disc image and your saves aren't changed, and you can turn the mod off at any time.
- Toy Town's suit works as before.

Made for Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032) and played there: Numemon became Monzaemon in Jijimon's House, any other partner got "It's empty.", and the suit was gone on returning as Monzaemon. The European release (all five languages) and the Japanese release have the same scripts and the mod finds and changes them, but it hasn't been played on those.

Needs Re:Compiled 1.6 or newer.
