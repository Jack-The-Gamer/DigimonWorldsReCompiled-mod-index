# Fast-Forward

Press **L2 + R2** together to play at **double speed**. Press them again for **triple speed**, and a third time to go back to **normal speed**. On the keyboard that's **Q + E**.

The game, its menus, walking, battles and training all speed up, but **the music keeps playing at its normal speed and pitch**, so it doesn't turn into chipmunk noise.

A **2x** or **3x** badge in the top-right corner shows when you're sped up.

**Options**

- **Sound effects**
  - **Normal pitch** (default): effects sound the same as at normal speed. They just come more often because the game is running faster.
  - **Sped up**: effects play faster and higher, like the rest of the game.

**Notes**

- L2 and R2 still reach the game the moment you press them, with no delay. Press them within about 1/8 of a second of each other and it counts as together; from then until you let go, the game doesn't see them.
- With L1, R1, Select or Start held, L2 + R2 goes to the game as usual, so the L1 + L2 + R1 + R2 + Select + Start reset still works. If you press the reset buttons a moment after L2 + R2, the speed goes back to what it was.
- Works alongside **Turbo**: Turbo lets L2 + R2 through whichever of the two is its turbo button.
- Movies are sped up along with their sound.
- If your computer can't keep up at 3x, the game runs as fast as it can and the sound may crackle. 2x is easier on slower machines.

Works in Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3. Needs Re:Compiled 0.2.6 or newer.
