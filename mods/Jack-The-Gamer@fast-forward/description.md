# Fast-Forward

Press **Select + Start** together to play at **double speed**. Press them again for **triple speed**, and a third time to go back to **normal speed**. On the keyboard that's **Backspace + Enter**.

The game, its menus, walking, battles and training all speed up, but **the music keeps playing at its normal speed and pitch**, so it doesn't turn into chipmunk noise.

A **2x** or **3x** badge in the top-right corner shows when you're sped up.

**Options**

- **Sound effects**
  - **Normal pitch** (default): effects sound the same as at normal speed. They just come more often because the game is running faster.
  - **Sped up**: effects play faster and higher, like the rest of the game.

**Notes**

- You can still use Select and Start on their own. The mod holds a lone press back for a moment (about 1/15 of a second) to see whether the other button follows, then passes it to the game.
- With L1, L2, R1 or R2 held, Select + Start goes to the game as usual.
- Movies are sped up along with their sound.
- If your computer can't keep up at 3x, the game runs as fast as it can and the sound may crackle. 2x is easier on slower machines.

Works in Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3. Needs Re:Compiled 0.2.6 or newer.
