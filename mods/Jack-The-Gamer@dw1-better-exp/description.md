# DW1 Better EXP

Your partner gets **twice the stat gains from gym training** and **three times the stat gains from battles**.

- **Training**: every exercise at every gym, both **Normal** and **Bonus Try**. The bigger numbers show up in the results window and are added to your partner. Skipping the training with Triangle gives the same gains.
- **Battles**: every won battle, whatever the stats it raises.

**Options**

- **Training gains** (default 2): how many times the normal gains a training gives, from 1 (unchanged) to 5.
- **Battle gains** (default 3): the same for battles, from 1 to 5.

**Notes**

- Only the stat gains change. Tiredness, weight, happiness and time spent work as before.
- The game's usual limits still apply: 9999 for HP and MP, 999 for the other stats.
- A failed Bonus Try normally gives half gains; with the mod you get the multiplied half.

Made for Digimon World (USA, SLUS-01032) and checked there. Boulder Moving gave +80 HP and +2 Offense without the mod and +160 and +4 with it. A battle against Dokunemon gave +1 MP, Offense, Speed and Brains and +2 Defense without the mod, and +3 and +6 with it. The mod looks for the game's code rather than relying on fixed addresses, so other releases of Digimon World may work too.

Needs Re:Compiled 0.2.6 or newer.
