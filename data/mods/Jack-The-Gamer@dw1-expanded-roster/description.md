# DW1 Expanded Roster

**Work in progress.** Digimon from Digimon World 2003 and Digimon World 2 join Digimon World, built from your own copies of those games each time the game starts (nothing of any of the games comes with the mod), along with Digimon World's own Tentomon, Goburimon, Gotsumon, Hagurumon, ToyAgumon, Tankmon, Tsukaimon, PlatinumSukamon, WaruMonzaemon, KingSukamon, WaruSeadramon and Machinedramon as Digimon you can raise, and a revised Digivolution tree with Megas.

## The revised Digivolution tree

Every Digimon in the mod evolves along a revised tree, not the game's own. Some changes:

- **Koromon** becomes Agumon, Guilmon, ToyAgumon, **Biyomon** or Hagurumon; **Tanemon** becomes Palmon, **Kunemon** (HP or Defense highest, weight 5 to 15), Tentomon, Gotsumon or Goburimon; **Tokomon** becomes Patamon, **Tsukaimon**, **DemiDevimon** or **Penguinmon**; **Tsunomon** becomes **Gabumon**, Veemon, **Betamon**, Elecmon (now at weight 5 to 15) or Renamon.
- **Whamon** is an **Ultimate**, from Coelamon, Seadramon or Shellmon, aiming for HP 4000, Defense 400, Brains 400, at most 10 care mistakes, weight 45 to 55, and discipline 70 or 30 techniques.
- **Palmon** becomes **Togemon**, **Woodmon**, Ogremon or Vegiemon, and **Vegiemon** becomes **Cherrymon** or **Lillymon**.
- **Devimon** can be raised from **Goburimon**, aiming for MP, Offense and Brains 100, **at least 10 care mistakes**, weight 25 to 35, and 30 battles.
- **Vademon** can be raised from **Nanimon**, aiming for MP 4000, Brains 500, at most 15 care mistakes, weight 15 to 25 and 35 techniques.
- Many Rookies and Champions have new or fewer evolutions (Agumon: Greymon, Monochromon, Tyrannomon; Greymon: MetalGreymon, SkullGreymon, Megadramon, Kimeramon; and so on), shown on the chart.

Every Digimon the tree names is now in the mod, so none of the game's old routes are left.

As with the game's own Digimon, the evolution is decided by how close your partner comes to these, so it doesn't have to meet every one.

For now the new Digimon:

- move with the animations of Digimon World's own Digimon (Agumon, Greymon, Tyrannomon, MetalGreymon, Gabumon, Leomon, Andromon, Kabuterimon, HerculesKabuterimon, Centarumon, Sukamon, Shellmon, Garurumon, Angemon, Ogremon, Frigimon, Betamon, MegaSeadramon, Giromon, Monzaemon, Etemon, Monochromon, Mamemon, Ninjamon, Unimon, Patamon), and play those motions for their techniques (their finishers have their own names and effects);
- keep those Digimon's care habits and voices;
- don't show on the Digivolution Chart yet once raised (a save has no room to record them so far); the chart itself is rebuilt with room for them and a Mega section, its panels reaching the window's edges so the icons stay as big as they can, and its detail page shows any number of evolutions and pre-evolutions.

With **DW1 Wild Digimon Randomizer**, the new Digimon can also turn up as wild Digimon. On a map each one loads as a lighter copy with only what a wild Digimon uses (its walking and battle motions and all of its techniques), about two thirds the size of the full Digimon, so it fits beside the area's other Digimon, and with the randomizer's random techniques it can fight with any of them. Expanded Roster keeps its own data out of the memory wild Digimon load into, so they have as much room as without it. In File City's Arena, a new Digimon in a tournament (your partner, or an opponent the randomizer brings into a grade cup) shows in the bracket with its own picture.
