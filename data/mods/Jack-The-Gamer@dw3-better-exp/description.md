# DW3 Better DV EXP

Every battle you win gives exactly **5 DV EXP**, instead of anywhere from 1 to 10.

Normal EXP is not changed: each battle still gives the same EXP as before.

- DV EXP goes to the same digivolutions as in the normal game (the ones used in the battle). The mod only changes how much they get.
- It is always 5, even where the game would have given more: once a digivolution's level is high enough, the game lets a battle give more than 10.

Made for Digimon World 2003 (SLES-03936) and checked there: in the same two battles, Greymon got 2 and 2 DV EXP without the mod and 5 and 5 with it, while Agumon's EXP went up by 5 and 6 either way. The mod looks for the game's DV EXP code rather than relying on fixed addresses, so Digimon World 3 (USA) should work too.
