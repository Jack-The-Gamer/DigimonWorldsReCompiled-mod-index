# DW3 Better Training

Training at Leomon's gym always succeeds.

Normally each of the three rounds of a training session can fail (about one time in four), and the bonus round only comes up if all three pass. With this mod every round passes, so you always reach the bonus round, and it passes too. You get the most out of every TP you spend.

- Works for every exercise and every TP amount (1, 5 or 10).
- How much a stat goes up in each round is still random, exactly as in the normal game.
- Nothing else about the game's randomness changes: battles, drops and everything else play out as before.

Made for Digimon World 2003 (SLES-03936) and checked there. It looks for the gym's training code rather than relying on fixed addresses, so Digimon World 3 (USA) should work too.
