# DW3 Auction Notifications

Never miss one of Asuka City's auctions again.

- **A notice when an auction opens.** "NEW AUCTION!" slides in at the bottom right of the screen, in the style of the game's BIT window, and slides away again after a few seconds.
- **A "!" on the world map.** While an auction is open, a yellow "!" stands over Asuka City on the map (Start, MAP).

The game holds sixteen auctions, in Cargo Tower in Asuka City, each at its own point in the story. One starts when you walk in, as long as you have the BIT it asks for. The first two ask for 800, later ones up to 32000. Once the story moves on, that auction is gone for good. This mod uses the game's own rule: the notice and the "!" mean "walk into Cargo Tower now and the auction starts".

- An auction that opens while you have too little BIT is announced when you have enough.
- The notice waits until you are back on the field, so it isn't lost on a black screen or an area title.
- Loading a save where an auction is already open shows the "!" on the map, but no notice.
- The "!" moves aside for the game's "you are here" bubble when you are in Asuka City, and hides while the cursor or the name of a place is over it.

Made for Digimon World 2003 (SLES-03936) and checked there (in its 60 Hz and 50 Hz modes), and on Digimon World 3 (Japan). It looks for the game's code rather than relying on fixed addresses, and the code and auction list are on the Digimon World 3 (USA) disc too, so it should work there as well.

Needs Re:Compiled 1.4 or later.
