# DW1 Un-Rigged Bonus Try

The Bonus Try slot machine at the gyms is rigged. Before each round starts, the game picks how it will treat you. In one of its moods it never lets you win: when the first two reels match and the third would match too, the third reel rolls one or two pictures past it. In another it lets you match anything except the jackpot, rolling past that instead. This mod takes that out.

**Options**

- **Slot machine**
  - **Original - No Rigging** (default): the game's own slot machine, including the times it nudges a reel onto a match or the jackpot for you, but it never rolls past a match on purpose.
  - **True Slots**: no nudging and no rigging. Each reel stops at the same moment after you press X as in the original, so only your timing decides what you get.

- Works in every gym's Bonus Try (both training overlays).
- Payouts are unchanged: three of the training's own picture, three of a kind, or the jackpot.

Tested in Digimon World (USA).
